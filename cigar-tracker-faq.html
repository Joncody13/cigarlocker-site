What these apps are

What is a cigar app?

An app for people who keep cigars. The useful ones do three jobs: they keep a record of what you own and where it is, they keep a journal of what you thought of each cigar, and they help you identify a cigar you are holding. Cigar Locker does all three.

What is a cigar scanner app?

An app that names a cigar from a photograph of its band or box, so you do not have to type the maker, line and blend yourself. Cigar Locker’s scanner is called Locker Vision. It names the cigar, fills in the blend from its catalog, and asks you to confirm before anything is saved. How the scanner works.

What is a humidor app?

An app that keeps the contents of your humidors: which cigars are in which humidor, how many are left, when you bought them and how long they have rested. How humidors work in Cigar Locker.

What is a cigar inventory app or cigar tracker?

The same idea under other names: a running count of your cigars that goes down when you enjoy, give away or move one. In Cigar Locker the count is worked out from each purchase’s history, so a gift is recorded as a gift and a move keeps the original purchase.

What is a cigar journal app?

A tasting diary. In Cigar Locker an entry has a rating out of ten stars, the flavors you noticed in each third, optional scores for construction, draw and burn, your notes and two photos. More about the journal.

Identifying a cigar

Can I identify a cigar by taking a picture of its band?

Yes. In Cigar Locker, photograph the band and review the suggested maker and line. Confirm the wrapper and size before adding it to your humidor.

I know the cigar brand. How do I find the exact cigar?

Search the catalog for the brand. Use any series name on the band or box to narrow the results, then compare the cigar’s length, shape, and ring gauge with the listed sizes.

Can I scan a whole cigar, or do I need a close-up of the band?

You can photograph the whole cigar. Face the band toward the camera so its lettering is legible. For a measurement, include the entire cigar and a U.S. bill or standard credit card for scale. If the band looks tiny in that photo, take a separate close-up for identification.

Can I identify an unbanded cigar that someone gave me?

A photograph of the leaf and shape rarely gives a reliable brand or blend. Ask the person who gave it to you for the box name or a photo of the label. Until then, record it as an unknown cigar.

Can I use a photo I already took?

Yes. Choose an existing photo in Locker Vision. Zoom in on the original first. If you can read the band or printed box label clearly, the scanner has a better starting point.

Which part of a cigar box should I photograph?

Start with the printed end or the lid. Look for the panel that names the line, size, and box count, and keep the entire label in focus.

Should I include both bands in the picture?

Yes. A secondary band or ribbon near the foot can distinguish two editions that use the same main band. Turn the cigar so both are readable.

Do I need to take the cigar out of its cellophane?

Try a photo through the cellophane first. If a reflection covers the lettering, change the angle or slide the cigar out far enough to photograph the band clearly.

Why won’t the scanner read a band I can see clearly?

Zoom in on the photograph itself. A band you can read in person may be only a few pixels wide in a shelf photo. Take a closer picture in even light and turn the lettering toward the lens.

What does “Possible match” mean on a scan?

The scanner found a candidate but the image leaves room for another identification. Check the alternatives against the band and box, or take a closer photo before choosing.

What can I do if Cigar Locker identifies the wrong cigar?

Tap “Think harder” to retry the photo. You can also search by name or enter the cigar manually. Review the match before saving it.

Can a band tell me whether the wrapper is natural or maduro?

Sometimes the wrapper appears on a secondary band or the box. When both wrappers share a band, check the original packaging or purchase record before confirming the wrapper in your collection.

Can the scanner identify a limited edition or a boutique cigar?

Include any edition name, year, or secondary band visible on the cigar or box. Cigar Locker can look up unfamiliar cigars, but a close match is worth checking against the maker’s release description.

Can a scan tell me the wrapper, binder, and filler?

After an identification, Cigar Locker looks up the documented blend and names where it came from. The binder and filler sit inside the cigar, so those details depend on matching the cigar to the right blend.

Can a scanner tell if a Cuban cigar is genuine?

A band match identifies the design. Authentication requires other evidence. If you have the original Habanos box, check its warranty-seal code through Habanos’ official authenticity service.

How can I find out which factory made my cigar?

Confirm the cigar’s line after scanning its band or box, then check the factory and country in its details; Cigar Locker names where each came from. If the maker lists several editions of the line, check the release year against your box.

What kind of cigar has a striped wrapper?

A spiral of contrasting tobacco leaves is often called a barber pole. For example, CAO America combines a dark Connecticut Broadleaf with a lighter Connecticut Shade leaf. Check the band and size to narrow down which striped cigar you have.

What’s the difference between Edición Regional and Edición Limitada on a Cuban cigar?

A Habanos regional edition was made for a particular market. Check the second band or box seal for the territory. A limited edition carries a release year on its black-and-gold marking. Include the territory or year when you record the cigar.

Why does the band on my Padrón look different from older photos?

Padrón introduced a one-piece guarantee band with an individual number on several lines, including the 1964 Anniversary Series, 1926 Serie and Family Reserve. Compare your band with the maker’s photos for that specific line.

How accurate is the scanner?

We publish no accuracy figure, because none has been measured on enough members’ scans for us to stand behind. It can be wrong, which is why you confirm every scan before it is saved.

How many cigars does Cigar Locker know?

Its catalog holds 4,600+ cigars in 1,200+ lines with blend details, from 200+ brands, and 800+ bands in its band library. A cigar it does not know is looked up on the web, which takes longer.

Scanning a shelf or box

Can I scan a whole humidor shelf in one photo?

Yes. Photograph one shelf with the bands or printed box ends facing you. Locker Vision lists the labels it can read. Check each match, size and quantity against the shelf before saving.

Can I enter several identical cigars without scanning each one?

Scan a representative cigar, confirm its identity and size, then enter the quantity you counted. Use separate entries when the wrappers or sizes differ.

Can I photograph several different loose cigars together?

Yes. Arrange them in one row with space between the bands and turn the labels toward the camera. Compare the results with the row, and take a close-up of any cigar the app misses.

Can a shelf scan count cigars hidden behind boxes or in a second layer?

A shelf photo captures visible labels. Lift the tray or check the second layer yourself, then confirm the physical count before adding or updating the quantity.

What happens if I scan a cigar I already own?

Review the match and confirm the additional quantity. The current iPhone app adds a matching scan to the existing cigar record. Double-check the humidor and quantity when recounting stock you already entered.

Can I scan a cigar photo from a computer?

Yes. Sign in to Cigar Locker on the web and open Locker Vision. Choose a photograph from your computer to review the identification at your desk.

Does the barcode on a cigar box also identify a single cigar?

A product barcode identifies the package it was assigned to. A box and a separately sold single can have different codes. If you’re logging one cigar from a box, use the box label to confirm the line and size, then enter one as your quantity.

Can Cigar Locker read a barcode?

Yes, on the iPhone. Point the camera at the striped barcode on a box or a cigar. A barcode it knows fills the cigar in; a new one is remembered with the cigar you save, so it is known the next time. Its barcode list is young, so many boxes will be new to it.

Length, ring gauge & vitolas

How do I measure cigar length and ring gauge with my phone?

Lay the whole cigar and a U.S. bill or standard credit card on the same flat surface. Take a photo straight down in Locker Vision, then compare its estimated measurements with the sizes offered for that line.

What does a cigar size like 6 x 52 mean?

In length-first notation, the cigar measures six inches long and 52/64 of an inch across, or about 20.6 millimeters in diameter. Some manufacturers print ring gauge first, so check the labels.

How do I calculate ring gauge with a ruler or caliper?

For a round cigar, measure the diameter across the straight body in inches and multiply by 64. A diameter of ¾ inch gives a 48 ring gauge. With a caliper, touch the sides lightly to avoid compressing the wrapper.

How do I measure the ring gauge of a box-pressed cigar?

Measure the length first and compare it with the maker’s listed sizes. A box-pressed cigar has flat sides, so measurements across its different faces can disagree. For an inventory record, use the maker’s published ring gauge for the confirmed size.

What if I don’t have a bill or card for scale?

You can still scan the band. Cigar Locker can offer the line’s known sizes for you to choose from. Use a ruler or the dimensions on the original box to choose yours.

What is a vitola, and why does it matter for identification?

A vitola describes the cigar’s size and shape. Two cigars can share a blend and band while being different vitolas. Record the size name and measurements when the maker provides them.

How can I tell a Padrón 2000 from a 3000 if the bands look alike?

Compare their measurements with Padrón’s own chart. The 2000 is 5 inches long with a 50 ring gauge. The 3000 is 5½ inches long with a 52 ring gauge.

Is a robusto the same size from every brand?

The name describes a general format, and the maker sets the precise dimensions. Check the length and ring gauge for the specific line before merging two inventory entries.

What do you call a cigar that tapers at one or both ends?

Figurado is a broad term for a shaped cigar. A pirámide or torpedo tapers toward the head. Habanos calls a cigar pointed at both ends a double figurado. A straight-sided cigar is a parejo. Use the maker’s size name when you can find it on the box.

Humidor inventory

How do I start a cigar inventory if I already have a full humidor?

Start with one shelf. Group matching cigars, count them, and record the maker, line, size and location. Add dates and prices from receipts when you have them. Leave an unknown purchase date blank.

Can I see which humidor a cigar is in?

Yes. Give each humidor a distinct name, such as Office Cabinet or Travel Case. Open a humidor to see its inventory, or check the cigar’s collection record for its location.

Can I track a tupperdor, cabinet and travel humidor in the same app?

Yes. Create a humidor entry for each storage location you use. Each gets its own name, photo and count, and the home screen shows the locations together.

Can I track boxes, bundles and loose singles in the same collection?

Yes. Record the packaging and quantity for each purchase. A five-pack can stay one entry with five cigars, while a single you picked up on a different day gets its own entry.

What should I record when I buy a box of cigars?

Keep the date, shop, price, quantity and humidor together. Copy the line, size and wrapper from the box label so you can identify the purchase after the box is empty.

How do I keep two boxes of the same cigar from different years separate?

Enter them as separate purchases. Each box keeps its own date, cost and remaining count, so you can tell which batch has rested longer.

What is a good way to organize the shelves in a large cigar cabinet?

Give each shelf or drawer a consistent label. Pick a system you’ll remember, such as maker or resting date, and take a shelf photo after rearranging it.

How do I move part of a box into my travel humidor?

Choose the purchase, enter the number you are moving and select the travel humidor. If you take five from a box of 20, the original humidor has 15 and the travel humidor has five. The original purchase date and price stay with both.

Can I move several purchases into a new humidor together?

Yes. Select the purchases you are moving, choose the destination and confirm. Check that the destination count matches the cigars you moved.

How can I tell how many cigars are left in an open box?

Open the humidor and look for that purchase. Its remaining quantity reflects the moves and removals you’ve recorded. Count the physical box if somebody may have taken a cigar without logging it.

How do I record a cigar I gave away, traded or damaged?

Choose the event that matches what happened to the cigar. A gift, trade or loss changes the inventory count and leaves a dated explanation. Save tasting notes for cigars you personally tried.

How do I fix a cigar I accidentally marked as gone?

Find the mistaken event in the cigar’s history and undo it. That corrects the count while preserving the original purchase details.

How often should I count the cigars in my humidor?

For a personal collection, try a count twice a year and after a big move. Count each purchase separately. If a count is off, look for an unrecorded gift, move or removal before adjusting it.

Can I search my collection by wrapper, country or strength?

Yes. Use those filters together or separately. For example, filter to Nicaragua and Maduro within one humidor to narrow the list to cigars you already have there.

Can I find cigars I own by their length and ring gauge?

Yes. Enter the dimensions in the collection search, for example 6 x 52. This helps when a box gives measurements but you cannot remember what the maker calls that size.

How do I track the different cigars in a sampler pack?

Find the maker’s list for that sampler and match each cigar by its band and size. Enter each distinct cigar separately, with its quantity and the same purchase date and shop. If you only know the pack price, keep that total in your notes and leave the individual prices blank.

Can a cigar inventory app handle thousands of cigars?

Cigar Locker has tested its inventory with 5,000 sample purchases representing more than 50,000 cigars. The collection loads in sections as you scroll. Those figures describe the developer’s test collection. Performance on your own device can vary.

How can I check what cigars I already own while I’m at a cigar shop?

Open your collection on your phone and search by brand, line or size. The remaining quantity tells you whether you already own it. Sorting by quantity also helps you see which cigars are down to their last few sticks.

Aging, storage & value

How can I tell which cigars have rested the longest?

Enter a date for each purchase and sort your collection by resting time. Two boxes of the same blend bought months apart can then be compared on their own dates.

Is the age of a cigar the same as the time it has spent in my humidor?

Those clocks can start on different days. A dated box can give you an earlier reference point, while your acquisition date tells you how long you’ve owned it. If you know both dates, record which is which.

What does “Ready now” mean for a cigar in my collection?

Its recorded resting time falls inside Cigar Locker’s suggested window for that cigar. Treat it as a planning aid. The blend, how you stored it and your own taste affect when you’ll enjoy it most.

What if I don’t know when I bought a cigar?

Leave the purchase date blank. If you later find a receipt or dated order confirmation, use that date so your resting-time comparisons start from a known point.

What humidity should I keep my cigars at?

Follow the maker’s guidance for the cigars you store. For example, Habanos recommends 65% to 70% relative humidity for its cigars, with a steady temperature of 16–18°C (about 61–64°F). Use a hygrometer to check your humidor.

Can Cigar Locker read my existing humidity sensor?

Not yet. Humidity sensors are coming soon. The readings shown in the app’s sensor demo are simulated. Use your hygrometer or its companion app for current conditions.

Why did the humidity in my humidor drop after I added cigars?

Drier cigars can absorb moisture from the air. Check the hygrometer’s accuracy and whether the humidification system is sized for the container before deciding what to change.

Can I see what I paid for a cigar and where I bought it?

Yes. Enter the shop and price on the purchase record. Keeping each purchase separate lets you look up the amount from that visit even if you buy the same cigar elsewhere later.

How does Cigar Locker estimate what my collection is worth?

It uses the value you set, then the price you paid if available, then an estimated value based on the maker’s suggested retail price. These figures describe your inventory and do not establish a resale price.

Why does my collection value cover fewer cigars than my inventory count?

Some cigars may have no price, personal valuation or available MSRP. The total identifies how many cigars it covers so you can see when part of the collection is unpriced.

Can Cigar Locker tell me how rare a cigar is?

For cigars with known production information, Cigar Locker can show a rarity score and explain its basis. Rarity and the amount you paid are separate pieces of information.

How do I read the date code on a Cuban cigar box?

Look underneath the box. On Habanos boxes packed since 2000, the date stamp uses a Spanish month abbreviation and a two-digit year. DIC 23 means the cigars were boxed in December 2023. The separate factory stamp identifies where they were made.

Does 1964 on a Padrón band mean the cigar is that old?

No. Padrón introduced the 1964 Anniversary Series in 1994 to celebrate 30 years in business. The maker ages the tobacco for four years. Record 1964 as part of the series name and use any documented box or purchase date to track your cigar’s history.

Humidor setup & humidity

Do I need a wooden humidor, or can I store cigars in a plastic container?

A clean food-storage container with a tight seal can serve as a tupperdor. Put the cigars and a two-way humidity pack inside, close the lid, and use a hygrometer to check conditions.

Can I keep a full box of cigars in its original wooden box?

Yes, if the whole box fits inside a humidity-controlled cabinet or humidor. The ordinary wooden box cigars are sold in offers physical protection, but on its own it gradually exchanges moisture with the room.

Do cigars in metal tubes still need a humidor?

Yes, for long-term storage. A tubo protects a cigar during travel and slows moisture loss. For Cuban cigars in aluminum tubes, Habanos recommends taking the cigar out when you put it in your humidor.

Can I keep cigars in my kitchen refrigerator?

Use a humidor or a sealed container with controlled humidity. A kitchen refrigerator can dry cigars out, and tobacco can pick up odors from food.

How do I season a new wooden humidor before adding cigars?

Check the instructions that came with your humidor first. A common method is to leave high-humidity seasoning packs in the empty humidor with the lid closed for about two weeks, then take them out and switch to your regular humidity packs before adding cigars.

My two hygrometers disagree. How can I tell which humidity reading to trust?

Place them side by side at a stable temperature and let both settle. If they still disagree, check each against a known humidity reference or a calibration kit. Follow the meter’s instructions before changing its calibration.

How many humidity packs do I need for a half-full humidor?

Follow the pack maker’s chart, and size the packs for the number of cigars the humidor can hold when full, not for what is in it today. A half-full humidor still has the same volume of air and wood to keep steady.

Can I put a 65% and a 69% humidity pack in the same humidor?

Pack makers advise one humidity level in each enclosed space. Mixing two gives the packs different targets to work toward. If you want different conditions for two sets of cigars, use separate containers.

Can I save cigars that dried out in an old humidor?

An intact cigar can regain moisture gradually in controlled storage. A sudden jump in humidity can split a brittle wrapper. If the cigars stayed dry for a long time, some of their original flavor may be gone for good.

Can Cigar Locker alert me when my humidor is too dry?

Not yet. Alerts will come with the sensors.

Importing & exporting

Can I bring my cigar inventory over from another app?

Yes, if the old app can export a CSV. In Cigar Locker, open Settings, then Your data, then Import a collection. Review the matches and quantities before confirming.

How do I import an Excel, Apple Numbers or Google Sheets cigar list?

Export the inventory sheet as a CSV and select that file in Import a collection. Include a header row so the importer can recognize the fields by name.

What columns do I need in a cigar inventory spreadsheet?

The maker is the only required field for a Cigar Locker import. Useful additional columns include line, size, quantity, humidor, purchase date, price and notes. A file without a quantity column counts each row as one cigar.

Can I import cigars if my spreadsheet doesn’t list a humidor?

Yes. Choose a destination for rows without a location during import. You can move groups of purchases into their proper humidors afterward.

Does the import price mean price per cigar or price for the whole box?

A column called Price could mean either. During review, the importer asks you to confirm how to interpret it. For a $200 box of 20, check that the value works out to $10 per cigar.

Will importing the same cigar spreadsheet twice create duplicates?

Importing an unchanged file again adds no new inventory. Review the import report to see which rows already exist, especially if you have edited the file since the first import.

What happens to old five-star or 100-point cigar ratings when I import them?

The importer uses the highest rating in your file to infer the scale. Five-point scores are doubled and 100-point scores are divided by ten. Review the results if your file contains only low scores.

Why did some of my spreadsheet rows or columns get skipped?

Check the import report. It lists unreadable rows and explains exclusions such as totals or empty lines. The review screen also names unfamiliar columns so you can rename their headings and try again.

Can I undo an inventory import if I picked the wrong file?

Yes, while the imported cigars have no later activity. Check the result immediately so you can reverse the entire import before recording moves or other changes.

How do I export my cigar collection or journal?

Go to Settings, then Your data, and choose the collection or journal export. Use the spreadsheet for editing or migration. A PDF is available if you want a readable copy to print.

Can I import ratings for cigars I’ve already finished?

A rated cigar with none left can be kept for its rating. Include the rating and an explicit quantity of zero in your file. Omitting the quantity column makes each row count as one cigar. Check the import report before treating the remaining counts as final.

Where can I get a cigar inventory spreadsheet template?

The Cigar Locker import guide includes a sample CSV to download that opens in Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets. Replace the example rows with your collection and keep the column headings. You can leave out fields you don’t track.

Tasting notes & ratings

How do I record which cigars I liked?

Create a journal entry for the cigar, give it a score from one to ten and add a short note about what you would want to remember next time.

How do I keep tasting notes for each third of a cigar?

Select the flavors you notice separately for the first, middle and last third. There are sixteen flavor options, plus a notes field for anything else you taste.

Can I score draw and burn separately from flavor?

Yes. Alongside your overall rating, you can give construction, draw and burn their own scores out of ten. Use the tasting notes to describe flavor in more detail.

Can I attach a photo of the band to my tasting notes?

A journal entry holds up to two photos. Use one for a close-up of the cigar or band and the other for anything you want to remember about the occasion.

How do I journal a cigar a friend gave me at a lounge?

Start a journal entry, scan the cigar or enter it by name, and mark it as a gift. You can log it directly without adding it to a humidor first.

Does logging a cigar change the count in my humidor?

Choosing one from your humidor for a journal entry deducts one cigar. If you picked the wrong one, undo the entry to put it back.

How can I tell whether a cigar tastes different after aging?

Try two cigars from the same box at different dates and write a journal entry for each. Compare your scores, flavors and notes on the cigar’s page. Recording the purchase date and storage conditions makes that comparison more useful.

Can I print or save my cigar journal with its photos?

Export the journal as a PDF to keep the photos with the entries. Choose the spreadsheet export instead if you want to sort or analyze the records.

Can a cigar app predict whether I’ll like a cigar?

After you rate at least three cigars, Cigar Locker can compare an unrated cigar to ones you scored. It shows an estimated score and the closest example from your collection.

Whose scores do I see in Cigar Locker?

The stars you record are your personal ratings. When the app estimates a likely rating, it uses your own history and labels the result as an estimate.

How can I turn a paper cigar journal into a digital journal?

Start with a cigar you want to remember. Enter its name, copy across your tasting notes, and attach a photo of the band or notebook page. Set the entry’s date to the day you originally had the cigar. Repeat for the entries you want to keep.

Can I update a cigar tasting note after I save it?

Yes. Edit the existing journal entry to add a detail you remembered or correct your notes. Editing the original entry keeps the change with that tasting and avoids logging the same cigar a second time.

If a cigar has chocolate or coffee tasting notes, does that mean it’s flavored?

Cocoa or coffee in a tasting note describes what the tobacco reminded the reviewer of. A cigar can have those notes naturally. If you want to know whether flavoring was added, check whether the maker describes that specific blend as flavored or infused.

Does a full-bodied cigar always mean a strong cigar?

No. Strength is how strong a cigar feels. Body describes how light or heavy it feels on the palate, while flavor describes what you taste and smell. In your journal you might describe the same cigar as rich in flavor, creamy in texture and mild in strength.

What does “finish” mean in a cigar review?

The finish is what lingers on your palate after a puff. Note the flavor you still notice and how long it lasts. For example, “brief pepper, then lingering cocoa” tells you more when you revisit the entry than a score alone.

Privacy & sharing

Are my cigar ratings public, or can I share just one entry?

Journal entries are private by default. You can choose to share an entry with all your added friends or select individual friends for that post.

Who sees a photo when I scan a cigar?

The photo is sent to Cigar Locker’s server and to the AI service that reads it, to identify the cigar. The privacy policy says what is kept from a scan and for how long. A photo you choose to save to your collection is kept with your account’s backup.

How do I keep my cigar collection private on a shared computer?

Sign out of the web app when you finish. Cigar Locker then clears the collection and photos it stored in that browser. Your account backup remains available the next time you sign in.

Can I turn off market research or partner emails?

Yes. Go to Settings, then Account. Market insights and partner emails have separate controls. The privacy policy explains which collection details are used for each choice.

How do I delete my account and remove the collection from my phone?

In Settings, open Account and choose Delete account to remove the account and its backup. Uninstall the app to remove the local collection as well. The privacy policy explains which records are retained.

What does Cigar Locker use my ZIP code for?

It uses your ZIP code for local weather and to open the lounge finder near your town. It may also use it for relevant emails and, according to your preferences, for market insights.

Do I need to be 21 to use Cigar Locker?

Yes. Cigar Locker is intended for adults 21 and older, and account setup includes an age check.

Can I block someone who sends unwanted messages?

Open the person’s profile in Friends and select Block or Report. To report an individual message or reply, press and hold it.

Words collectors use

Short definitions of the terms that turn up on bands, boxes and in this app.

TermWhat it means
BandThe paper ring around a cigar that carries the maker’s name and emblem. Some cigars wear a second band or a ribbon at the foot.
LineA named blend from a maker, sold in several sizes.
VitolaA cigar’s size and shape, by name (Robusto, Toro) and by figures (5 x 50).
Ring gaugeA cigar’s thickness, in 64ths of an inch. A 50 ring cigar is 50/64 of an inch across.
WrapperThe outer leaf. It gives a cigar its color and much of its character.
BinderThe leaf under the wrapper that holds the filler together.
FillerThe leaves at the center of the cigar. Long filler means whole leaves run the cigar’s length.
Natural and MaduroTwo wrapper shades a line is often sold in: Natural is lighter, Maduro is darker and longer fermented.
PuroA cigar whose wrapper, binder and filler all come from one country.
ParejoA straight-sided cigar with a rounded head. Robusto, Toro, Corona and Churchill are parejos.
FiguradoAny shape that is not straight-sided: tapered or bulging, such as a Torpedo, Belicoso, Perfecto or Salomón.
Box-pressedPressed square in the box, so the cigar has flat sides.
TuboA cigar sold in its own tube.
Cap, head and footThe head is the end you cut, closed by the cap. The foot is the end you light.
StrengthHow strong a cigar is, usually given from mild to full.
HumidorA box or cabinet that keeps cigars at a steady humidity.
TupperdorA sealed food-storage container used as a humidor.
HygrometerThe instrument that reads the humidity inside a humidor.
Relative humidityThe moisture in the air as a percentage, which is what a hygrometer reads.
Resting or agingLeaving cigars in a humidor for months or years before enjoying them.
MSRPThe maker’s suggested retail price. Cigar Locker uses it only to estimate what a collection is worth.
Regular production, limited editionWhether a cigar is made year after year or in one run. Cigar Locker uses it for a cigar’s rarity score.
