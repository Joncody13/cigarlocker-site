For shops

Reach opted-in adult cigar collectors

Cigar Locker members keep their humidors in the app, cigar by cigar, and some choose to receive offers from shops by email. We choose who receives your members' deal inside Cigar Locker, from what they keep and enjoy. Your shop never receives their details.

How a deal works

You give us a code or a link for something you want to move: an overstock, a new arrival, a line you'd like more people to try. You set the members' discount.

We email it to members who have said yes to offers from shops, across the country or near you. And, if you like, it goes out by itself to a member the day they finish the last of that brand, with a line like "Last time you paid $240 for 20."

You sell as you always do, on your own site, under your own terms, with your own age check and shipping rules. Nothing changes at your end, and nothing is sold or shown inside the app itself.

You see what came of it. For each email: how many received it, opened it, clicked, put something in the cart, and bought. Carts and sales are counted with one line of code on your site (here is how), or through your affiliate network if you have one.

Cigar Locker is paid a share of the sales made with your code, agreed with you, settled monthly from your own figures or the network's report. No fee to join, nothing up front.

Who gets the emails

Only members 21 and over who chose to receive offers from shops. Each email carries why they got it, an unsubscribe link, and our postal address. Members in places where tobacco coupons are not allowed are left out automatically. Your shop never receives a member's name or address: they arrive on your site like any other visitor, carrying your code.

Featured placement

The app also has a map of cigar shops and lounges, found and checked by Cigar Locker, where members mark the places they recommend. A shop can appear first for its area, clearly marked Sponsored, with its logo and photo. Available once the app has been approved by the app stores.

Two rules

A deal is a discount, never a free cigar: the FDA forbids free tobacco samples. And every order is yours: you verify age and follow shipping law as you do for every customer.

Start with one deal

Send the deal in a line, the code, the link and the dates to jonathan@cigarlocker.app. It can be in members' inboxes within a day.
