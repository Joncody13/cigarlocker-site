8. Estimates may be wrong

Much of what Cigar Locker shows is worked out by software, including AI, and is an estimate. Please check anything that matters to you. In particular:

  • Identification: a scan's name for a cigar may be wrong, especially from a band that is small, unusual or hard to read.
  • Measurements: lengths, ring gauges and sizes measured from a photo are approximate.
  • Facts: blends, factories, release years, sizes and availability come from makers' catalogs and the web, and sources sometimes disagree or are out of date.
  • Prices and value: the maker's suggested price and any collection value are estimates. They are not appraisals, quotes or offers, and not a price anyone will pay or charge.
  • Rarity, likely ratings and aging: a rarity score, the rating the app thinks you would give a cigar, and how long it says a cigar should rest are our own rough guides.
  • Humidity: the sample sensors show pretend readings, to show how it will work. They say nothing about your own humidors, and Cigar Locker does not watch your humidors or alert you. Don't rely on it to protect your collection.
  • Suggestions and pairings: these are ideas for your enjoyment, not recommendations you must follow.
