Suggest One

Three from your collection, with a reason.

Tell it where you are, whether you have eaten and how much time you have. Suggest One picks three cigars from your own humidors and explains each choice. Going away? It helps you decide what to pack.

  • Pairing SelectorDrinks and food for any cigar, or the cigars in your humidor that suit a drink.
  • Packed for the tripName the town and the dates, and it chooses from your collection with the forecast in mind.

02

Places

Shops and lounges, anywhere.

Type a town, or tap Near me, to see its cigar shops and lounges on a map. Members recommend the places they like and mark what each is known for. Every listing is found and checked by Cigar Locker.

  • Recommended by membersRecommend a place, mark that you have visited, and say what it does best: a walk-in humidor, a comfortable lounge, knowledgeable staff.
  • Before you travelSearch the town you are headed to and see its shops and lounges before you go.

Make it yours

11 layouts. Two themes: Reserve and Cellar.

A layout is how your home screen is arranged, from the photo-led Portrait to the all-type Ledger. A theme is its colors: Reserve is dark, Cellar is light.

Reserve, the dark theme Cellar, the light theme

Your collection

Always with you.

On your phone first

Your collection opens instantly and works with no signal, even in the humidor room.

Backed up to your account

Sign in to back up your collection and photos, and to bring them to a new phone or the web.

Friends you choose

Nothing is posted publicly. You decide which friends see what you are enjoying, and you can block anyone at any time.

Yours to take

Export your collection and journal to a spreadsheet whenever you like, or delete your account in the app.

Scanning, look-ups, suggestions, Places and Friends use our server, so they need you to be signed in. Read about your privacy.
