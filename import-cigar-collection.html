Import a collection

Bring your collection with you.

A collection you already keep somewhere else does not have to be typed in again. Cigar Locker imports a spreadsheet file from another app, accounts for every line in it, and can undo the import with one tap.

Try Cigar Locker on the web

Free to try, with nothing to install: it runs in your browser and you sign in with Apple. It is laid out for a computer’s screen first. For adults 21 and over.

How to import

  1. In your old app or spreadsheet, export or save your collection as a CSV file. An Excel or Numbers workbook is saved as CSV first.
  2. In Cigar Locker, open Settings, then Your data, then Import a collection, and choose the file.
  3. Check what it found, choose the humidor for lines that name none, and bring it in.

What it reads

Columns are found by their names, in any order. Cigar Locker reads, when the file has them:

  • The maker, the line, the size name, the length and the ring gauge.
  • The wrapper, binder, filler, country and strength.
  • How many, your rating, and the humidor.
  • The date bought, the price, the shop, and whether it was a box or singles.
  • Your notes.

A column it does not understand is named on the screen, so you know what was left out.

The details that trip up an import

  • Headings. Maker may be headed Brand, Manufacturer or Maker; the line may be Line, Name or Series; the size Vitola or Size; the count Quantity, Qty or Count; the place Humidor or Location. Columns can be in any order, separated by commas, semicolons or tabs.
  • Ratings. The highest rating in the file tells Cigar Locker the scale: out of 5 is doubled, out of 10 is kept, out of 100 is divided by ten.
  • Dates. 2026-03-14, 3/14/2026, 3/14/26, Mar 14 2026 and 14 Mar 2026 are all read. A date that cannot be read, or one in the future, is left blank and you are told which line.
  • Prices. A column headed plainly Price could mean one cigar or the whole line. Cigar Locker makes a first guess from the amounts and asks you to confirm which it is before anything comes in.
  • No count column. Each line is then taken as one cigar.
  • Totals rows and blank lines are set aside and listed with the reason, not imported.

Every line is accounted for

Each line of the file ends up as one of these, and the report says which: brought in; already in your collection; kept for its rating with none left; set aside for you to check; or not readable, with the reason. The totals add up to the number of cigars in the file. The report can be saved as a spreadsheet.

Nothing is doubled, and it can be undone

  • The same file brought in twice adds nothing the second time.
  • Two boxes of one cigar bought on different dates stay two purchases, each with its own date and price.
  • One tap undoes the whole import, until something happens to the imported cigars.

And out again

Your collection and your journal can each be exported to a spreadsheet or a PDF at any time. A collection exported from Cigar Locker can be imported back whole.

Questions about importing

Can I move my collection from another cigar app?

Yes, if that app can export your collection as a spreadsheet (CSV) file. Cigar Locker reads the columns by name, so the file does not need a particular layout.

What file types does the import read?

Spreadsheet files saved as CSV, with commas, semicolons or tabs between the columns. An Excel or Numbers workbook is saved as CSV first.

What if my file does not say which humidor each cigar is in?

Everything goes into the humidor you choose, and Cigar Locker then offers ways to sort it: check off several purchases and move them together, or photograph a shelf to place what is on it.

Can I get my data out of Cigar Locker?

Yes. Settings, then Your data, exports your collection and your journal, each as a spreadsheet file.
