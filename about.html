About Cigar Locker.

Cigar Locker is a cigar collection, inventory and journal app for adults 21 and over. It identifies a cigar from a photo of its band or box, keeps every humidor and purchase in order, and gives each cigar you enjoy a page in a tasting journal. It does not sell tobacco.

What it is not

  • It is not a shop. No cigars or tobacco can be bought in or through Cigar Locker, and the app shows no shop prices, offers or links to stores.
  • It is not a ratings publication. The ratings in it are each collector’s own.
  • It is not the other kind of cigar locker: the rented humidor locker at a lounge, or a humidor cabinet. Cigar Locker is an app.

What it does

  • Identifies a cigar from a photo of its band or box, and measures it beside a dollar bill or a card.
  • Keeps the collection: every purchase with its date, price and shop, and what is left.
  • Keeps every humidor, and moves cigars between them.
  • Keeps a tasting journal: ten stars, flavors by third, notes and photos.
  • Imports a collection from a spreadsheet, and exports it.
  • Runs on the web at a desk, with the same collection.
  • And more: Suggest One, Places, Friends, and eleven home screen layouts in two themes.

Where its cigar facts come from

Cigar Locker’s catalog is built from what makers publish about their own cigars: their websites and catalogs, and trade catalogs that reprint them. It keeps the source of each fact, and for a fact checked against a maker’s own page, the page and the day it was read.

Where two sources disagree, both are kept and the difference is put on a list for a person to settle; nothing is overwritten by a guess. A scan teaches the catalog what a band looks like only after a member has saved the cigar under that name.

What we do not claim

Cigar Locker is new. We publish no scan accuracy figure, no member or download count, and no rating, because none has been measured and documented yet. When one is, it will appear here with how it was measured.
