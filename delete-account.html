Delete your account

You can delete your Cigar Locker account and the data our server keeps for it at any time, in the app or by asking us.

In the app

Open Cigar Locker, go to Settings > Account and tap Delete account, then confirm. It is done at once.

Without the app

Email support@atlantisdev.ai from the address your account uses (the one Apple or Google gave us when you signed in), with the subject Delete my Cigar Locker account. If you used Apple's Hide My Email, tell us your username in Friends instead, if you made one. We confirm it is you, delete the account within 30 days, and write back when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile (date of birth, ZIP code, and any name, phone and address you gave) and email choices.
  • Your backup: humidors, shops, cigars, purchases, history and journal, and every photo in it.
  • Your username, friends, posts, replies, messages and their photos.
  • Your Places recommendations, "been here" marks and tags, and your sensors' latest readings.
  • Your scan and look-up counts, and the record of how you used the app.

A Cigar Locker Plus subscription is not cancelled by deleting the account: cancel it in your Apple ID settings or in Google Play first.

What may be kept

  • Reports other members made about your content, with your account removed from them, to keep Friends safe.
  • Shared libraries that are not linked to you: band descriptions learned from scans and cigar facts looked up for everyone.
  • Records the law requires us to keep, such as those about a purchase, for as long as it requires.
  • Short technical logs, which are deleted on their own within days.

The collection on your phone stays there until you delete the app. The privacy policy says more.
