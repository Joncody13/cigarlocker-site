Emails

Email preferences

To stop marketing emails from Cigar Locker, use the Unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we sent you. You don’t need to sign in.

Two other ways

  • In the appOpen Settings, then Account. Under Emails, turn off News from Cigar Locker, Emails from Cigar Locker partners, or both.
  • By emailWrite to support@cigarlocker.app from the address our emails come to, and we’ll turn them off for you.

Essential account, security and billing emails aren’t marketing, so they still come.
