For the collector's humidor

Your humidors, beautifully kept.

Cigar Locker is a collection, inventory and journal app for iPhone. Scan a band or a box and it names the cigar, measures it and looks up the rest. Then it keeps every humidor, every shelf's humidity and every journal entry in one quiet, handsome place.

What it does

Everything a collector keeps track of, and a few things you couldn't.

01

Scan a band or a box

Take a photo, or choose one already in your photos. Cigar Locker reads the band the way a collector does, by its colors, shape and emblem as well as its words, along with the wrapper's color and the shape of the head. It names the cigar and fills in the form for you. A box gives the count and packaging too.

Lay the cigar beside a dollar bill, a credit card, a business card, a quarter or a ruler and it measures the length and ring gauge, then settles on a size that line is actually made in. If the name isn't right, Not correct? Think harder takes a closer look, or choose from the close matches it offers.

02

The homework, done for you

After a scan it looks up the wrapper, binder and filler, the factory and its town, the country, the year the line was released and every size it comes in, and names where the facts came from. Many lines are answered straight from the makers' own catalogs.

Each cigar gets a rarity score out of 10, and where you didn't note what you paid, the maker's suggested price stands in, always marked as an estimate.

03

Every humidor, every purchase

Keep as many humidors as you own, in as many houses. Each purchase remembers where it came from, when, whether you bought it in person or had it shipped, and why you chose it, if you like.

Move part of a box and it keeps its date and price. Gifts, trades and damage are noted as what they are, and any line in a cigar's history can be undone. Search, filter by strength, wrapper, country, length or rarity, sort any way you like, and export it all to a spreadsheet.

04

Humidity, shelf by shelf

Connect your SensorPush sensors and see the humidity and temperature in each humidor. A tall cabinet with several sensors is drawn as a map, each reading in its place, green when it's on target and amber when it drifts.

Give each shelf its own target, see the average or every sensor, and look back by the day, week, month or year. Needs a SensorPush WiFi Gateway; readings are checked while the app is open.

05

A journal worth keeping

Rate each cigar out of 10 stars, write your tasting notes, and keep two photos: one of the cigar, and one of the moment, where you were and who you were with.

Every entry has its own page, can be edited later, and stays yours unless you choose to tell friends.

06

Suggest One

Not sure which to reach for? Say whether you're inside or out, how long it should last, how strong, and whether you've eaten. Suggest One weighs the hour and, outside, the weather at your ZIP code, then offers three from your own humidor, each with its reason.

Planning a trip? Say where and when, and it packs a list from your collection to suit the forecast there.

07

Pairings, both ways

Drinks and food to go with any cigar you keep, always including a choice without alcohol. Or start from a drink or a dish and see which cigars in your humidor suit it best.

08

Friends, and only friends

Add friends by username or with an invite code. Tell them what you're enjoying, with a photo if you like, and choose exactly who sees it. Reply, message, and share a friend's post with others.

Nothing is public. Hide your posts from anyone, choose whether people can find you, and block or report anyone at any time.

09

Make it yours

Eight home screen styles, from the photo-led Private Reserve to the all-type Ledger, and five palettes: Onyx, Midnight, Emerald, Cognac and Oxblood, each in light, dark or automatic.

10

Bring your collection with you

Kept a list in another app? Save it as a spreadsheet file and Import a collection reads it, offers to fix any spellings, never adds a cigar twice, and can be undone.

Private by design

Yours, and nobody else's.

Your collection lives on your iPhone first, so it opens instantly and works with no signal in the humidor room.

  • Sign in with Apple to back up your collection and photos privately, and bring them back on a new phone.
  • Your SensorPush password stays in your iPhone's Keychain and goes only to SensorPush.
  • No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. Your personal data is never sold.
  • Delete your account inside the app whenever you like.

Scanning, look-ups, suggestions and Friends use our server, so they need you to be signed in. Read the privacy policy.
