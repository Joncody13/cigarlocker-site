How scanning works

Scan the band. Know the cigar.

Cigar Locker reads a band the way a collector does: its colors, shape and emblem as well as its words.

  1. 1Photograph itA band, a box or a whole shelf. Lay a dollar bill or a card beside a cigar and it is measured too.
  2. 2Cigar Locker identifies itIt names the cigar, then fills in the blend and the sizes the line comes in.
  3. 3You confirm, then saveCheck the size and wrapper with a tap, or correct them. Nothing is saved until you do.
  • Your likely ratingAn estimate from the cigars you have rated, with the reason.

02

The collection

Everything you keep, at a glance.

Every humidor and every purchase in one place: how many you have, where each one is, what you paid and how long it has rested. Your home screen opens on the count.

  • Value, clearly labeledWhat you paid, your own value, or an estimate at MSRP. Each figure says which.
  • Resting time and aging windowsHow long each purchase has rested, and when it is likely at its best.
  • Bring your collection with youImport a spreadsheet from another app. Every line is accounted for, and one tap undoes it.
  • Yours to takeExport your collection and journal to a spreadsheet whenever you like.

03

Humidors

Every humidor, in order.

Keep as many humidors as you own. See what is in each one and how long it has rested, and move cigars between them without losing a purchase's date or price.

  • Move part of a boxIt keeps its date, its price and its history. Move several at once, and undo a move if you change your mind.
  • Place cigars by photoPhotograph a shelf, and cigars listed in another humidor move here once you confirm them.
  • Humidity sensors Coming soonCigar Locker's own sensors are on the way. Until then the app has a sensor demo, labeled as a demo, with simulated readings.

Shown: the app's sensor demo. Its readings are simulated.

04

The journal

Evenings worth keeping.

Rate each cigar out of ten, tap a few flavors for each third, and add two photos: the cigar and the moment. Each entry gets a page of its own.

“Cocoa and cedar in the first third, then black pepper. A long finish.”

  • A history for every cigarGifts, trades and moves are recorded as what they are, and any of them can be undone.
  • Shared when you chooseFriends see an entry only when you decide to post it.

Desktop sync

At your desk, too.

Sign in at cigarlocker.app with the same account and your collection is there on a larger screen: a dashboard, tables you can sort, and Locker Vision, the same scan with room to work. A change you make on one shows up on the other, usually within a few minutes.
