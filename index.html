01

The scan

Point. Scan. Known.

Cigar Locker reads a band the way a collector does: its colors, shape and emblem as well as its words. It names the cigar, measures it against a dollar bill or a card, and settles on a size the line is actually made in.

  • Reads the bandEven the ones that never say what they are.
  • Measures itLength and ring gauge, from the photo.
  • Does the homeworkWrapper, binder, filler, factory and the year it was released.

Humidity

Every shelf, in its place.

Connect your SensorPush sensors and a tall cabinet is drawn as a map, each reading where it hangs: green on target, amber when it drifts. Give every shelf its own target, then look back by the day, week, month or year.

03

The journal

Evenings worth keeping.

Rate each cigar out of ten stars, write what you tasted, and keep two photos: the cigar, and the moment. Every entry has its own page, and stays yours unless you choose to share it.

“Cocoa and cedar from the first inch, then black pepper. A long finish.”

  • Every cigar's historyGifts, trades and moves noted as what they are, and undone with a tap.

04

Suggest One

The right cigar for the hour.

Inside or out, the weather at your ZIP code, whether you've eaten, how long you have. Suggest One weighs it all and offers three from your own humidor, each with its reason. Going away for the weekend? It packs for the trip.

  • Pairings, both waysDrinks and food for any cigar, or the cigars that suit a drink.
  • Try NextLines you don't have yet, chosen from your own ratings.
