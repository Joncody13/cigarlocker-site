On the web

Your collection, at a desk.

Cigar Locker runs in a web browser as well as on a phone. Sign in at cigarlocker.app with the same account and your collection is there on a larger screen, laid out for a desk.

Try Cigar Locker on the web Try Cigar Locker on the web

Free to try, with nothing to install: it runs in your browser and you sign in with Apple. It is laid out for a computer’s screen first. For adults 21 and over. The iPhone app is coming soon to the App Store.

What a desk adds

  • A dashboard. The count, each humidor as a card, what was added lately and what is inside its rule-of-thumb resting window, on one screen.
  • Tables that sort. The whole collection as a table: click a heading to sort, filter by humidor, strength, wrapper, country and more, and search from the keyboard.
  • Move many at once. Check the purchases in a humidor and move them together.
  • The journal in three panes. Every entry, the entry you are reading, and the cigar it is about, side by side.
  • Locker Vision. The same scan, with photographs chosen from the computer and room to check what it found.

One collection, two places

The web app and the phone app are the same account and the same collection. A change you make on one shows up on the other, usually within a few minutes.

What you need

  • A current desktop browser with JavaScript on. Chrome is the browser we test most.
  • A Cigar Locker account. On the web you sign in with Apple.
  • Nothing to install. A browser on a shared computer keeps nothing after you sign out.

What stays on the phone

Scanning a row with the camera and subscribing to Cigar Locker Plus are done in the phone app. The sensor demo is on the phone too.

Questions about the web app

Is there a desktop version of Cigar Locker?

Yes. Cigar Locker runs in a web browser at cigarlocker.app/app, laid out for a desk, with the same collection as the phone app. There is nothing to install.

Does it work on a Mac and on Windows?

It runs in the browser, so it does not depend on the computer. Use a current desktop browser; Chrome is the one we test most.

Does the web app cost extra?

No. It is part of Cigar Locker and uses the same account.

Do I need the phone app to use the web app?

No. You can sign in on the web and keep a collection there. The phone app adds the camera for scanning a row.
