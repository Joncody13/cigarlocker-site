Press kit

For reviewers and the press.

Everything needed to write about Cigar Locker: a description, the logo, screenshots and the facts. Write to us for anything else.

In one sentence

Cigar Locker is a cigar collection, inventory and journal app for adults 21 and over that identifies a cigar from a photo of its band.

In one paragraph

Cigar Locker is an app for people who keep cigars. Photograph a band or a box and it names the cigar, fills in its blend and helps confirm its size. It keeps every humidor and every purchase, with the date, the price and the shop, counts what is left, and gives each cigar you enjoy a page in a tasting journal. The same collection is on the web at a desk. Cigar Locker is made by Atlantis Dev, LLC. It is for adults 21 and over, and it does not sell tobacco.
