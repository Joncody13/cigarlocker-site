      Deals from shops

      A shop’s special, by email only, never in the app. Email it to everyone who said yes to offers, anywhere in the country, or to the group on the People tab; or let it go by itself to a member the day they run out of its brand. Carts and sales come back as for any email: from a shop that reports them, an affiliate report pasted on the email’s page (Emails tab), or totals typed in there.

        Add a deal

        {first name}, {brand} (what they ran out of, as they write it) and {last paid} (what they paid last time, “$240 for 20”). A paragraph with {last paid} is left out for anyone who never typed a price, so keep it in a paragraph of its own. It goes only to members who said yes to offers, never twice for one deal, and no one gets more than one of these a week.

        Never send offers to

        States (FL), ZIP codes (02903) or the first three digits of a ZIP code (100), with spaces between. Members there get no offers at all. Started with Providence and New York City, where tobacco coupons are banned; your lawyer should set this list.

        Kind of email

          For a deal, {brand} is its brand as each person writes it, and {last paid} what they paid last time (“$240 for 20”); a paragraph with {last paid} is left out for anyone who never typed a price.

          Write {first name} for each person's first name ("there" when they gave none). A blank line starts a new paragraph. Paste a shop's web address to link to it, or put [Shop the offer](https://shop.com/deal) on a line of its own for a button. Each email ends with why they got it, an unsubscribe link and your postal address.
