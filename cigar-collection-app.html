Collection and inventory

A cigar collection, kept in order.

Cigar Locker is a cigar collection and inventory app. It records each purchase as you made it, counts what is left in each humidor, and keeps the history of every cigar that has come and gone.

Try Cigar Locker on the web

Free to try, with nothing to install: it runs in your browser and you sign in with Apple. It is laid out for a computer’s screen first. For adults 21 and over.

Built around the purchase

A collection is a set of purchases, not a list of names. Each purchase in Cigar Locker keeps how many you bought, the date, what you paid, the shop, and whether it was a box, a bundle or singles. Two boxes of the same cigar bought a year apart stay two purchases, each with its own date and price.

Counts that stay right

What is left is worked out from what happened: added, moved, enjoyed, given away, traded, lost. A gift is recorded as a gift, never as a journal entry. Part of a box can move to another humidor and keep its date and price. Any of it can be undone.

An example. You buy a box of 20 in March. Over the summer you move 5 to a travel humidor, give 2 to a friend and enjoy 4. Cigar Locker shows 9 left in the cabinet and 5 in the travel humidor, all still dated March and priced from that box, and the box’s history lists each of those steps in order.

Search, filter and sort

  • Search by maker, line, size or wrapper, with the words in any order. A size typed as figures, such as 6 x 52, finds cigars of that size.
  • Filter by humidor, strength, length, how long a cigar lasts, wrapper, country and rarity.
  • Sort by newest, name, how many are left, your rating, rarity, or which have rested longest.

Prices, values and estimates

A money figure is always one of three things and says which: what you paid, a value you set yourself, or an estimate at the maker’s suggested retail price. A total says how many cigars it covers when that is not all of them. Cigar Locker shows no market value and no shop price.

Resting time, rarity and your likely rating

  • How long each purchase has rested, beside a rule-of-thumb resting window. The window is guidance, not a prediction.
  • A rarity score for a cigar whose production is known, with the reason.
  • Your likely rating for a cigar you have not rated: an estimate from the cigars you have rated, with the reason, always marked as an estimate.

The resting rule of thumb it uses

Collectors disagree about aging, and no rule fits every cigar. So that “how long has it rested” means something, Cigar Locker shows a window by strength. It is guidance, not a guarantee: it is our rule of thumb and not a maker’s instruction, and how a cigar was stored, its blend and your own taste all change the answer.

StrengthRule-of-thumb resting window
Mild2 to 12 months
Mild to medium3 to 18 months
Medium6 to 24 months
Medium to full9 to 36 months
Full12 to 48 months

A dark wrapper, such as a maduro or a broadleaf, adds a year to the far end. A light one, such as a Connecticut shade, starts two months sooner and ends six months sooner. Past the window the app says only that, and adds that your own taste decides.

Inside the window the app says “Ready now”. That is shorthand for “inside its rule-of-thumb window”, not a promise about how the cigar will taste.

Large collections

Lists are drawn a screen at a time, so a large collection stays quick to open and to search. We test Cigar Locker with a made-up collection of 5,000 purchases, more than 50,000 cigars. That is a test of ours on our own equipment, not a promise about every phone.

Yours to keep and to take

Your collection lives on your phone and works with no signal and no account. Signing in backs it up and puts it on the web at a desk. You can import a collection from a spreadsheet, and export your collection and journal to one whenever you like.

Questions about keeping a collection

What is the difference between a cigar collection app and an inventory app?

In practice they are the same thing: a record of which cigars you own, how many, and where they are. Cigar Locker adds what an inventory alone leaves out: each purchase’s date, price and shop, and a history of where every cigar went.

Does Cigar Locker track what I paid?

Yes, for each purchase, if you enter it. Where you did not, it can show an estimate at the maker’s suggested retail price, marked as an estimate. It never shows a shop’s price or a market value.

Do I need an account to use it?

Not to keep your collection, humidors and journal on your phone. An account is needed for backup, for the web app, and for the parts that use our server, such as scanning.

Can Cigar Locker be used to buy cigars?

No. Cigar Locker does not sell cigars or tobacco, shows no shop prices or offers, and links to no store. It is a collection and journal app for adults 21 and over.
