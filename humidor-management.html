What is in each humidor

Each humidor has a page of its own: its photo, how many cigars it holds, and every purchase in it with how many are left. The home screen shows each humidor with its count, and a collection can be viewed one humidor at a time.

Moving cigars

  • Move part of a box. It keeps its date, its price and its history.
  • Move several at once. Check the purchases, choose the humidor, and they move together. A move can be undone.
  • Place cigars by photo. Photograph a shelf, and cigars listed in another humidor move here once you confirm them.

Humidity sensors: coming soon

Cigar Locker’s own humidity sensors are on the way. They are not available yet, and the app does not read any other maker’s sensors today. Until ours arrive, the app has a sensor demo, labeled as a demo, with simulated readings, so you can see how a humidor’s humidity will be shown.

When the sensors are released this page will say so, with what they measure and what they cost. We will not describe them as available before they are.

Keeping a humidor well, with or without an app

  • Choose a humidity target and keep to it. Many collectors aim somewhere in the 60s, as a percentage of relative humidity; steadiness matters more than the exact figure.
  • Note the day cigars go in. Resting time is counted from that day.
  • Give each humidor a name that says where it is. A collection kept in two houses is two humidors.
  • Count what is there twice a year and correct the record. A record nobody trusts is not used.

Questions about humidors

Can I keep more than one humidor in Cigar Locker?

Yes, as many as you own, each with its own name, place and photo.

Does Cigar Locker monitor humidity today?

Not yet. Cigar Locker’s own humidity sensors are coming soon. Today the app has a sensor demo whose readings are simulated and labeled as a sample.

Which humidity sensors does it work with?

None yet. Cigar Locker will work with its own sensors when they are released.

What happens to a purchase’s date when I move cigars?

Nothing. Cigars moved to another humidor keep the date, price and history of the purchase they came from.
